# Reader Paths

This public reading guide helps readers start from their purpose and reach the strongest relevant sequence in the Sutton SignWriting eight-series release.

The paths are reader aids, not separate claim lanes. Substantive citation should still use the platform DOI, a series DOI, or an artifact DOI according to the claim being made.

## Shared Core Path

Use this path for any reader who wants the common foundation before choosing a specialized route.

1. *Language Without Sound* - Sutton SignWriting Foundations
2. *The Shape of Writing* - Sutton SignWriting Foundations
3. *The Four Language Pathways* - Sutton SignWriting Language and Literacy Research
4. *Brazil as the Empirical Center of SignWriting* - Sutton SignWriting Evidence and Adoption
5. *Evidence Boundaries and Adoption Limits* - Sutton SignWriting Evidence and Adoption
6. *The Shape of a Sign* - Formal SignWriting

## New Reader

Use this path for general readers, journalists, students, and collaborators who need the core idea first.

1. *Start Here* - Sutton SignWriting Foundations

3. *The Shape of Writing* - Sutton SignWriting Foundations
4. *What the Current Evidence Base Shows* - Sutton SignWriting Evidence and Adoption

## Editorial Rule

Reader paths should stay practical and directional:

- name the reader's purpose
- point to the first useful document
- show the next few steps
- avoid turning the path card into an abstract or defense
- keep community authority, participant review, technical validity, standards status, data governance, and stewardship as separate layers
