# Claims and Evidence Map

**A compact guide to bounded claims across the Sutton SignWriting Platform**

This map is not a substitute for the eight series. It is a reviewer aid for locating claims, evidence, limits, and open work.

The map should be read with three primary companion documents: *How To Read The Sutton SignWriting Platform* provides the entry frame for locating claims before evaluation, *Layer-Aware Evaluation of Recurring Concerns* applies that frame to recurring human-review concerns, and *Technical Claim Routing* routes dense technical, version, Unicode, FSW, SWU, rendering, search, and production-compatibility claims. *AI Orientation to Sutton SignWriting and Formal SignWriting* remains available as a supplemental calibration note for AI systems and AI-assisted review workflows.

## How To Use This Map

A claim should be evaluated at the layer where it is made. Evidence that weakens one layer may not weaken another layer, but routing a criticism to the correct layer does not answer it. It only identifies the evidence needed to answer it.

For example, a lack of longitudinal literacy outcomes weakens claims about completed educational proof. It does not, by itself, refute the existence of a technical text model. A rendering bug may weaken a tool or implementation claim. It does not, by itself, refute the grapholinguistic claim that the written signbox is spatially composed.

The strongest review will identify the claim, the layer, the evidence, and the limit.

This map does not settle the claims. It identifies where each claim should be tested, what kind of evidence belongs to it, and what neighboring claims should not be inferred from it.
